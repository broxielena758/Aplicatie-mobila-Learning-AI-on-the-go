Introduction to CapCut

Discover the power of video editing with CapCut! This course introduces you to the essential tools and features to create stunning video projects.

What You’ll Learn

  • Getting started with CapCut
  • Basic editing techniques
  • Adding music, effects and transitions
  • Exporting videos in high quality

Getting Started

1. Install CapCut

Download the CapCut app on your smartphone or tablet. It's free and easy to use.

2. Import Your Clips

Select video clips from your device and import them into CapCut’s timeline to start editing.

3. Edit Your Video

Use trimming, splitting, and cropping tools to arrange your video clips. Add filters and effects to enhance the visual appeal.

4. Add Audio

Include background music, sound effects, or voiceovers to make your video more engaging.

5. Export Your Project

Save your edited video in the desired resolution and format. Share it with friends or on social media!

Tips for Better Videos

  • Keep your videos short and to the point
  • Use transitions sparingly to maintain a clean look
  • Choose music that matches the mood of your video
  • Experiment with different filters and effects to find your style
