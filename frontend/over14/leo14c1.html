How to Use ChatGPT

1. Ask Open-Ended Questions

Type detailed and clear questions to get the most helpful responses.

2. Customize Outputs

Provide examples or specify the tone (formal, casual, etc.) for tailored results.

3. Practice Interviews

Simulate interview scenarios by asking ChatGPT to play the role of an interviewer.

Ethics and Best Practices

  • Use AI as a supplement, not a replacement, for critical thinking
  • Always verify information provided by AI
  • Avoid sharing sensitive or private data
