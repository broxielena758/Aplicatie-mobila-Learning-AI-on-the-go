Introduction to Mentimeter

Mentimeter is an interactive presentation tool that allows you to engage your audience with live polls, quizzes, and Q&A sessions. This course will teach you the basics of using Mentimeter for your projects.

What You’ll Learn

  • Setting up a Mentimeter account
  • Creating interactive presentations
  • Adding polls and quizzes to your slides
  • Tips for engaging your audience

Getting Started with Mentimeter

1. Sign Up

Create a free account on Mentimeter’s website to get started.

2. Choose a Template

Select a pre-designed template or create your own presentation from scratch.

3. Add Interactive Elements

Include polls, quizzes, or word clouds to make your presentation more engaging.

4. Present and Share

Share the presentation link with your audience and watch their responses in real-time!
